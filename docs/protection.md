# PIN protection and hidden applications

Open **Preferences > PIN and hidden applications** and configure a 4-8 digit PIN.
This protects preferences and shortcut creation. Select the applications and
folders that should also require it when opened.

Hidden entries disappear from home, folders and application pickers, together
with shortcuts targeting their package. Their assignments remain saved and
return when unhidden, unless you replace their tiles.

**Disable protection** reveals hidden entries and stops PIN prompts while
retaining your selections. Five incorrect attempts pause verification for
30 seconds. The PIN is stored as a salted verifier, never as clear text.

If you forget the PIN, clear LauncherTV's data in Android's application settings.
This also erases the home layout and preferences.

Protection applies to navigation through LauncherTV. It is not Android kiosk
mode: other launchers, system settings or external applications may still open
protected applications or clear LauncherTV's data.
