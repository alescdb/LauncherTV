Simple TV Launcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy applies to Simple TV Launcher (org.cosinus.launchertv), an Android home-screen application developed by Alexandre Del Bigio.

Data collection and sharing

Simple TV Launcher does not send personal data to the developer or operate a developer-run server. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not request Android's Internet permission. You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Data used on your device

The app reads information about installed launchable applications, including their names, package names and icons, to display and open them. Your home-screen layout and preferences are stored in the app's private storage.

Depending on your version and the features you enable, this storage can also contain folders, shortcuts, hidden or locked application selections, and an image you choose for the home screen. If you enable PIN protection, the app stores a salted derived value for checking the PIN, rather than the PIN in plain text.

Optional status displays use the device's battery status, network connection state and local IP addresses. These values are displayed on your device and are not sent to the developer. The app does not use location services to determine your location.

Permissions and other applications

The wallpaper permission allows the app to offer system wallpaper controls. Network-state access supports the optional connection display. Selecting a home image uses Android's file picker to access the image you choose.

When you open another app, system settings, a shortcut or an external website, that destination handles your interaction under its own policies. Simple TV Launcher does not control the data practices of those destinations.

Storage, backup and deletion

Local settings and files remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Clearing storage resets the launcher's configuration. Android backup and device-transfer services may copy or restore app data according to your device settings and the operating system's policies. The developer does not receive or control these backups; manage them through your device or backup provider.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information and does not provide advertising or accounts.

Contact and policy changes

For questions about this policy, contact Alexandre Del Bigio through the project's issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include sensitive personal information.

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
